## Getting Started

### System Requirements
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Internet connection
- User account credentials from your administrator

### Accessing the System
1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: `http://your-clinic-domain.com` (or provided URL)
3. You'll see the login page

---

## User Registration & Login

### First-Time User Registration
*(Only if you don't have an account yet)*

1. **Click "Register"** on the login page
2. **Fill in your information:**
   - **Full Name**: Your complete name
   - **Email**: Your work email address
   - **Phone**: Your phone number
   - **Password**: Create a strong password (8+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password
3. **Select your role** (if applicable):
   - **Doctor**: Medical professionals
   - **Receptionist**: Front desk staff
   - **Admin**: System administrators
4. **Click "Register"**
5. **Check your email** for verification (if required)
6. **Log in** with your new credentials

### Daily Login Process
1. **Go to the clinic system URL**
2. **Enter your email/username**
3. **Enter your password**
4. **Click "Remember Me"** (optional, for your personal device)
5. **Click "Login"**

### Forgot Password
1. **Click "Forgot Password?"** on login page
2. **Enter your email address**
3. **Check your email** for reset link
4. **Click the link** in the email
5. **Create a new password**
6. **Log in** with your new password

## Patient Management

### Adding a New Patient

1. **Click "Patients"** in the navigation menu
2. **Click "Add New Patient"** button
3. **Fill in Patient Information:**

   #### Personal Information
   - **First Name**: Patient's first name
   - **Last Name**: Patient's last name
   - **Email**: Patient's email (optional)
   - **Phone**: Patient's phone number
   - **Date of Birth**: Select from calendar
   - **Gender**: Male, Female, or Other
   - **Address**: Street address, city, state

   #### Medical Information
   - **Medical History**: Previous conditions, surgeries
   - **Allergies**: Known allergies (medications, food, etc.)
   - **Medications**: Current medications
   - **Blood Type**: A, B, AB, O with +/- factor

   #### Emergency Contact
   - **Contact Name**: Emergency contact person
   - **Relationship**: Relationship to patient
   - **Contact Phone**: Emergency phone number

4. **Click "Save Patient"**

### Finding an Existing Patient

#### Method 1: Search
1. **Click "Patients"** in navigation
2. **Use the search bar** at the top
3. **Type** patient name, email, phone, or patient ID
4. **Press Enter** or click search icon

#### Method 2: Browse
1. **Click "Patients"** in navigation
2. **Use filters** (optional):
   - **Status**: Active, Inactive, Archived
   - **Registration Date**: Date range
   - **Age Range**: Min/max age
3. **Browse the patient list**
4. **Click on a patient** to view details

### Viewing Patient Details

When you click on a patient, you'll see:

#### Overview Tab
- **Basic Information**: Name, contact, demographics
- **Medical Summary**: Key medical information
- **Recent Activity**: Recent visits and results

#### Visits Tab
- **Visit History**: All past visits
- **Upcoming Visits**: Scheduled appointments
- **Add New Visit**: Schedule a new visit

#### Service Results Tab
- **All Results**: Test results and findings
- **Status Indicators**: Draft, Pending, Approved
- **Add New Result**: Enter new test results

#### Billing Tab
- **Bill History**: All invoices and payments
- **Outstanding Balance**: Amount due
- **Payment History**: Payment records

#### Appointments Tab
- **Scheduled Appointments**: Future appointments
- **Past Appointments**: Completed appointments
- **Schedule New**: Book new appointment

### Editing Patient Information

1. **Go to patient's profile**
2. **Click "Edit Patient"** button
3. **Update the information**
4. **Click "Save Changes"**

## Visit Management

### Scheduling a New Visit

1. **Go to the patient's profile**
2. **Click "Schedule New Visit"**
3. **Fill in Visit Details:**
   - **Visit Date**: Select date from calendar
   - **Visit Time**: Select time slot
   - **Reason for Visit**: Brief description
   - **Notes**: Additional information

4. **Select Services:**
   - **Individual Services**: Choose specific tests/consultations
   - **OR Select Package**: Choose a pre-defined package
   - **System will show pricing** automatically

5. **Click "Schedule Visit"**

### Managing Visit During Patient Arrival

1. **Find the patient** (using search or patient list)
2. **Go to their profile**
3. **Find today's visit** in the Visits tab
4. **Click "Check In"** (if available)
5. **Update visit status** as needed

### Recording Services During Visit

1. **Go to the visit details**
2. **Click "Add Services"** (if not pre-selected)
3. **Select services provided:**
   - **Lab Tests**: Blood work, urine tests, etc.
   - **Imaging**: X-rays, ultrasounds, etc.
   - **Consultations**: Doctor consultations
4. **System automatically calculates total cost**
5. **Click "Save Services"**

### Completing a Visit

1. **Ensure all services are recorded**
2. **Enter any visit notes**
3. **Click "Complete Visit"**
4. **System will:**
   - Generate bill automatically
   - Update patient records
   - Mark visit as completed

## Service Results

### Understanding Result Types

The system supports three types of results:

1. **Text Results**: Written findings, descriptions, narratives
2. **Numeric Results**: Numbers, measurements, lab values
3. **File Results**: Documents, images, PDFs, X-rays

### Adding Service Results

#### Method 1: From Patient Profile
1. **Go to patient's profile**
2. **Click "Service Results" tab**
3. **Click "Add New Result"**

#### Method 2: From Visit
1. **Go to the visit details**
2. **Click "Add Results"** for each service

### Entering Different Result Types

#### Text Results
1. **Select the service** from dropdown
2. **Result type will auto-detect** as "Text"
3. **Enter the result text** in the text area
4. **Add notes** (optional)
5. **Click "Save Result"**

#### Numeric Results
1. **Select the service** (e.g., Blood Test)
2. **Result type auto-detects** as "Numeric"
3. **Enter the numeric value**
4. **Add unit** (if applicable, e.g., mg/dL)
5. **Add reference range** (optional)
6. **Click "Save Result"**

#### File Results
1. **Select the service** (e.g., X-Ray)
2. **Result type auto-detects** as "File"
3. **Click "Choose File"** or drag & drop
4. **Select the file** (PDF, JPG, PNG, etc.)
5. **Wait for upload** to complete
6. **Add description** (optional)
7. **Click "Save Result"**

### Approving Results (Doctors/Admins Only)

1. **Go to Service Results section**
2. **Find results with "Pending Approval" status**
3. **Review the result details**
4. **Click "Approve"** or "Reject"**
5. **Add comments** (if rejecting)
6. **System notifies patient** (if configured)

### Viewing Results

#### Patient View
1. **Go to patient's profile**
2. **Click "Service Results" tab**
3. **Click on any result** to view details
4. **Files can be downloaded** or previewed

#### Timeline View
1. **Go to patient's profile**
2. **Click "Timeline" tab**
3. **See chronological view** of all activities
4. **Results show with icons** indicating type

## Billing & Payments

### Understanding Bills

Bills are automatically generated when visits are completed. Each bill includes:
- **Bill Number**: Unique identifier (BILL-XXXXX)
- **Patient Information**: Patient name and ID
- **Itemized List**: Services provided with prices
- **Total Amount**: Sum of all services
- **Payment Status**: Draft, Sent, Partial, Paid, Overdue

### Viewing Bills

1. **Click "Bills"** in navigation
2. **Browse bill list** or search for specific bills
3. **Click on a bill** to view details

### Processing Payments

#### Cash Payment
1. **Go to bill details**
2. **Click "Make Payment"**
3. **Select "Cash"** as payment method
4. **Enter amount received**
5. **Click "Process Payment"**
6. **Give receipt** to patient

#### Card Payment
1. **Click "Make Payment"**
2. **Select "Card"**
3. **Enter card details:**
   - **Card Number**: 16-digit card number
   - **Expiry Date**: MM/YY format
   - **CVV**: 3-digit security code
4. **Enter amount**
5. **Click "Process Payment"**
6. **Wait for approval**
7. **Print receipt** if successful

#### Bank Transfer
1. **Select "Bank Transfer"**
2. **Enter transfer details**
3. **Mark as pending** until confirmed
4. **Update status** when funds received

### Managing Outstanding Bills

1. **Go to "Bills" section**
2. **Filter by "Unpaid" or "Partial"**
3. **Contact patients** for payment
4. **Update payment status** when received

### Sending Bills to Patients

1. **Go to bill details**
2. **Click "Send Bill"**
3. **Choose delivery method:**
   - **Email**: Send to patient's email
   - **Print**: Generate printable version
   - **SMS**: Send bill summary (if configured)

## Services & Packages

### Understanding Services

Services are individual medical procedures or consultations:
- **Lab Tests**: Blood work, urine analysis
- **Imaging**: X-rays, ultrasounds, MRIs
- **Consultations**: Doctor visits, specialist appointments
- **Procedures**: Minor medical procedures

Each service has:
- **Name**: Clear description
- **Price**: Current cost
- **Category**: Type of service
- **Result Type**: Expected output format
- **Duration**: Estimated time required

### Viewing Services

1. **Click "Services"** in navigation
2. **Browse by category** or search
3. **View service details** including pricing
4. **See service status** (active/inactive)

### Managing Services (Admins Only)

#### Adding New Service
1. **Click "Add Service"**
2. **Fill in service details:**
   - **Service Name**: Clear, descriptive name
   - **Description**: Detailed explanation
   - **Price**: Current cost
   - **Category**: Service type
   - **Result Type**: Text, Numeric, or File
   - **Duration**: Time in minutes
3. **Click "Save Service"**

#### Updating Service Information
1. **Find the service** in the list
2. **Click "Edit"**
3. **Update information**
4. **Click "Save Changes"**

### Understanding Packages

Packages are bundles of services at discounted prices:
- **Basic Checkup**: Essential health screening
- **Comprehensive Checkup**: Complete health assessment
- **Women's Health**: Female-specific services
- **Executive Checkup**: Premium health screening

### Viewing Packages

1. **Click "Packages"** in navigation
2. **Browse available packages**
3. **See included services** and pricing
4. **View package details** and benefits

### Using Packages in Visits

1. **When scheduling visit**, select package instead of individual services
2. **System shows all included services**
3. **Price is automatically calculated**
4. **Can add additional services** if needed

## Common Workflows

### Workflow 1: New Patient Registration and First Visit

1. **Register New Patient:**
   - Click "Patients" → "Add New Patient"
   - Fill in all required information
   - Save patient

2. **Schedule First Visit:**
   - Go to patient profile
   - Click "Schedule New Visit"
   - Select date, time, and services
   - Save visit

3. **Patient Arrives:**
   - Find patient in system
   - Verify information
   - Check in for visit

4. **During Visit:**
   - Record services provided
   - Update visit notes
   - Complete visit

5. **After Visit:**
   - System generates bill automatically
   - Process payment
   - Schedule follow-up if needed

### Workflow 2: Processing Lab Results

1. **Receive Lab Results:**
   - Go to "Service Results"
   - Find pending results for the service

2. **Enter Results:**
   - Click "Add Result"
   - Select patient and service
   - Enter result data (text, numeric, or file)
   - Save result

3. **Review and Approve:**
   - Doctor reviews result
   - Click "Approve" or "Reject"
   - Add comments if needed

4. **Patient Notification:**
   - System notifies patient of approved results
   - Patient can view results in their portal

### Workflow 3: End-of-Day Closing

1. **Review Today's Visits:**
   - Check all visits are completed
   - Verify all services are recorded
   - Ensure all results are entered

2. **Process Payments:**
   - Check for unpaid bills
   - Process cash/card payments
   - Update payment records

3. **Generate Reports:**
   - Run daily revenue report
   - Review patient visit statistics
   - Check for any issues

4. **Prepare for Tomorrow:**
   - Review tomorrow's appointments
   - Prepare patient files
   - Check for any special requirements

## Tips & Best Practices

### Daily Operations

#### Before Clinic Opens
- **Check today's schedule**: Review appointments
- **Prepare patient files**: Gather necessary documents
- **Verify equipment**: Ensure everything is working
- **Check supplies**: Stock necessary materials

#### During Clinic Hours
- **Update records in real-time**: Don't wait until end of day
- **Double-check patient information**: Verify accuracy
- **Communicate with team**: Share important updates
- **Handle payments promptly**: Don't let bills accumulate

#### End of Day
- **Complete all records**: Finish data entry
- **Process payments**: Handle all transactions
- **Back up data**: Ensure system backup
- **Prepare for tomorrow**: Set up next day's work

### Patient Communication

#### Best Practices
- **Always verify patient identity**: Ask for name and DOB
- **Explain procedures clearly**: Patients should understand what's happening
- **Be transparent about costs**: No surprise charges
- **Follow up on results**: Ensure patients receive their results

#### Privacy and Confidentiality
- **Never share patient information**: Except with authorized personnel
- **Log out when done**: Especially on shared computers
- **Secure physical records**: Keep files locked away
- **Follow HIPAA guidelines**: Medical privacy laws

### System Usage

#### Data Entry Tips
- **Save frequently**: Don't lose work due to timeouts
- **Use search efficiently**: Learn to find patients quickly
- **Double-check entries**: Review for accuracy
- **Use templates**: For common entries

#### Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Can't Find Patient:**
- Check spelling of name
- Try different search terms (phone, email, patient ID)
- Check if patient is marked as "inactive"

**System is Slow:**
- Close unnecessary browser tabs
- Clear browser cache
- Check internet connection
- Contact IT support if persistent

**Payment Processing Issues:**
- Verify payment details
- Check internet connection
- Try alternative payment method
- Contact payment processor if needed

### Keyboard Shortcuts

#### Navigation
- **Ctrl + K**: Quick search
- **Ctrl + N**: New patient/record
- **Ctrl + S**: Save current form
- **Esc**: Close modal/dialog

#### Form Navigation
- **Tab**: Move to next field
- **Shift + Tab**: Move to previous field
- **Enter**: Submit form (when in last field)
- **Ctrl + Enter**: Submit form (from any field)

### Mobile Usage

The system works on mobile devices with these considerations:

#### Best Practices
- **Use Wi-Fi when possible**: More stable than cellular
- **Keep software updated**: Latest browser versions
- **Enable auto-rotate**: Better form visibility
- **Use larger text**: Easier to read

#### Limitations
- **Some features may be limited**: Complex forms work better on desktop
- **File uploads**: May be slower on mobile
- **Printing**: Not available on mobile

A: Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page and follow the instructions.

A: Yes, if your administrator allows remote access.

A: Most records can be edited. Find the record and click "Edit".

A: Contact IT support immediately. Use paper forms temporarily.

A: The system backs up automatically, but notify IT of any critical data.
